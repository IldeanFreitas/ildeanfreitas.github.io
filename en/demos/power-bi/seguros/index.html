Execution in Power BI: none. HTML prototype with fictional data; each page has a "Como seria no Power BI" (how it would be in Power BI) panel with visuals, DAX measures and model, sourced from Microsoft Learn or marked "a confirmar" (to be confirmed).

Fictitious scenario

Baluarte sells 4 lines of insurance through branches. Discounts above the approval limit, claims past the settlement deadline and overdue audit findings are concentrated in a few places, but the monthly report shows only the network average.

What the dashboard shows

A governance dashboard with exception rate by branch and line, discount inside and above the approval limit, an example loss ratio, late claims by month and audit findings by days overdue and severity.

Try it in 3 steps

  1. On the cover, click Ir ao ponto crítico: Serra (go to the critical point: Serra).
  2. On Sinistros (claims), click a month with delays and watch the other visuals filter.
  3. In Ver como (view as), pick Analista de sinistros Auto (motor claims analyst) and open Como seria no Power BI (how it would be in Power BI) to see the access rule by line.
