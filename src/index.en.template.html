About

I understand the process before proposing the solution

The foundation is infrastructure: hardware, networks and six years managing the systems of a wholesale and retail company, responsible for the ERP, the database, replication, backup, the firewall and the network and hardware support teams.

In 2017 I moved into development on the Power Platform. Since then I've worked across retail, distribution, mining, the public sector and consulting. Today I'm a senior software development analyst at Compass UOL, on projects that combine Power Platform and Business Intelligence over SAP Datasphere, SAP BW, SAP HANA, Analysis Services and Azure Databricks.

My education followed the same order as my career: a bachelor's degree in Information Systems, a postgraduate degree in IT Governance, an MBA in Data Science and Big Data Analytics and a postgraduate degree in Data Engineering and Architecture with an Emphasis on AI.
