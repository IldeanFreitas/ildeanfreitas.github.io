  2. August 2023 – May 2024

    Software Development Analyst (mid-level) | Federal public administration

    Through a consulting firm, for a federal public administration body. More than 10 applications in Power Apps on Dataverse and SharePoint, with Power Automate flows and custom connectors, plus Power BI dashboards. Brasília, DF.

  3. July 2020 – August 2023

    Software Development Analyst | Mining

    Built the corporate Data Warehouse of a mining company, covering 10 areas: human resources, finance, inventory, logistics, billing, geology, laboratory, material movement, fleet telemetry and machinery operating hours. The model unified the ERP, vehicle tracking and fuel control, which until then only existed separately. In parallel, more than 15 applications in Power Apps and the automations that fed them. Power Automate, Power BI and Report Builder on SQL Server and Firebird. Marabá, PA.

  4. November 2017 – March 2020

    Software Development Analyst | Wholesale and distribution

    Introduced Power Apps and Flow (now Power Automate) at a distributor: more than 10 applications that digitized processes previously run outside any system, integrated with the TOTVS ERP and Máxima's field sales platform. Decision-support reports for directors, managers and the field sales team. IT environment support and administration of SQL Server, Oracle and Firebird. Marabá, PA.

  5. April 2016 – August 2017

    Software Development Analyst | Supermarket retail

    Led the ERP replacement of a hypermarket chain across 4 stores (headquarters and 3 branches), each with 60 checkouts, to WinThor/TOTVS, completed in 3 months. Project manager role: requirements, acceptance, cutover from the previous system and post-go-live support. Trained more than 200 employees in checkout operation, finance, accounts payable and receivable, purchasing and inventory movement. Parauapebas, PA.
